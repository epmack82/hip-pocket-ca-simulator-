# Campaign preview 4.5.0

Local implementation only. No deployment, API service, student login, external feedback collection, or schoolhouse document publication is part of this change.

## What changed

- New campaigns offer more candidate destinations than available field visits: 3-day drill = 3 of 6, 14-day annual training = 7 of 11, 29-day deployment = 10 of 16. These adjustable authored exercise budgets include abstract travel/coordination/reporting time; they are not real operational movement estimates.
- No new seven-day campaign option. Existing saved scenario pools and legacy visit allowances are preserved without regeneration.
- Role selection explicitly names difficulty, retains stars, and explains guidance/responsibility. Team Chief starts with a five-part pre-deployment planning rehearsal. The written recommendation is saved, shown in mission planning, and exported for AAR review. It is not full MDMP, an approved COA, an actual personnel selector, or an automatically graded command plan.
- A schematic destination board provides a visual selection aid. Positions are not real geographic relationships. Remaining visit slots are explicit. Unvisited destinations appear in the leader report as handover gaps, not automatic failures.
- Full submission receipts, copy/download controls, and earlier scene submissions allow players to recover what they wrote. Continue Scene replaces Back to Location. Validation questions appear before the continuing narrative.
- Create a Product opens a centered native modal dialog. Short briefs capture type, recipient, purpose, and key finding/uncertainty. Required-field participation earns reporting credit once per site; it does not establish accuracy, approve an action, send a real document, or assess a full template. Older worksheets retain their completion scores. Rubric label/version changed to Reporting practice / local-evidence-2.
- Template Workshop is separate from mission scoring and saves drafts locally. It reuses existing app-authored worksheets. Raw schoolhouse documents and extracts are not included.
- Unknown clauses are logged locally in the playtest report. Manual flags persist in mission saves. Nothing is transmitted automatically: the player must export and share the file with the administrator.

## Interpretation and narrative changes

The Major's compound example is now split at action-leading commas. Data correction is not classified as an apology. Proposed meetings, bid requests, data reconciliation, and public communications receive distinct reactions that preserve their pending status. No project approval, corrected population figure, published announcement, or completed meeting is fabricated.

Interpreter orientation and ordinary market engagement no longer trigger interpreter substitution merely because the phrase contains “have our translator give.” Rapport intention is distinguished from demonstrated sentiment change. Existing explicit substitution/exclusion and misconduct tests still pass.

Openings and apologies now have fuller authored dialogue; prior incidents continue to require mitigation. These are fictional design rules and character responses, not claims about Thai people or quoted doctrine. The implementation remains a bounded local parser and authored consequence system, not general natural-language understanding. New phrasing can still be unresolved or misclassified; exported feedback remains important.

## Verification and limitations

Automated coverage includes full three-visit completion with unused destinations, all campaign pool sizes, Chief plan save/reload, exact legacy seven-day preservation, short-product submission/repeat-credit protection, local feedback, standalone workshop drafts, and the reported compound-action/interpreter regressions. Production build and browser checks cover the selection, planning, action receipt, continuation and product dialog flows. No live site was published.

Full MDMP/COA wargaming, actual team selection, comprehensive cultural modeling, geographic maps, scene illustrations, and unrestricted conversational understanding are not implemented by this preview. Source release review and doctrine validation remain separate work; the local reference library is not consulted by the app at runtime.
