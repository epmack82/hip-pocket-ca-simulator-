## Exact prompts

### Market arrival

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: wide arrival artwork bundled inside an offline Civil Affairs training game. Create a restrained, richly textured painted field-journal illustration of a fictional contemporary Southeast Asian neighborhood market, Thailand-inspired but not an actual place. Eye-level view along a public market lane with ordinary low shopfronts, canvas awnings, produce stalls, adult vendors going about everyday work, handcarts and a small delivery vehicle in the distance. A few back-row shutters are closed while front stalls trade, without implying why. Warm afternoon light, muted ochre, teal and slate, believable everyday dignity, cinematic environmental storytelling, no combat or fantasy equipment. Wide landscape composition suitable for a scene banner; keep key content across the middle. No lettering, labels, logos, flags, insignia, map markings, children, dramatic disaster, armed people, or depicted political/ethnic conflict. This is atmosphere, not evidence of a hidden event.

### mali

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: single square character portrait for a fictional offline training game. A fictional middle-aged Thai woman, named Mali in the game, a contemporary neighborhood market association chair, wearing an ordinary muted teal cotton blouse. Head and shoulders, attentive composed expression with restrained warmth, facing viewer. Painterly field-journal realism, textured brushwork, soft warm afternoon light, ochre and deep slate background with indistinct market awnings. She is a specific individual, not an ethnic archetype. No lettering, labels, insignia, logos, ceremonial clothing, weapons or stereotyped gestures. No real person likeness. Opaque background.
